ATLAS 1
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: FINAL production icon atlas, one square PNG with true alpha transparency.
Reference: the supplied approved 2x2 sample is a STYLE AND MATERIAL reference only. Change its layout to a STRICT 4x4 EQUAL-SQUARE grid and use the exact new subjects below. Preserve its luxurious maximalist nacre relief, luminous iridescent mother-of-pearl surfaces, ornate silver-white baroque scrollwork, sculpted fine ivory lace, raised pearl beads and exquisitely carved feathers. Do NOT simplify to generic plain porcelain or flat symbols.
Layout is crucial: exactly 4 columns and 4 rows of equal-sized square cells. Cell centers at 12.5%,37.5%,62.5%,87.5% horizontally and vertically. Center EVERY whole icon at its cell midpoint. Each COMPLETE object including all ornaments, halos, ribbons, wings and shadows MUST stay within CENTRAL 75% of its cell. This leaves 12.5% transparent padding around every cell edge. Plenty of transparent gutters and transparent outside margins. No overlapping neighboring icons, no crossing cell boundaries. Do not let a long ribbon extend outside the cell. Match visual scale for the whole collection.
Orientation: EVERY icon is FRONT-FACING and UPRIGHT, straight-on orthographic relief. Rectangular objects have perfectly vertical sides and horizontal tops. No diagonal camera, isometric view, rotation or tilted composition. Depth is shown only by frontal sculpted relief and material highlights.
Aesthetic: dreamy sacred angel sanctuary, ornate pearl white and cool silver, subtle nacre pink/lilac shimmer, no gold dominance. Fine cool silver outlines keep white objects legible on a white web background. Soft frontal light, no background shadows. Elaborate yet immediately recognizable functional silhouettes.
Motif variation is required: SOME icons have wings/halos, SOME have small white crosses and lace, SOME have white roses/vines/pearls as specified. Do NOT put wings on every icon. All share the same nacre, baroque silver-white craftsmanship.
Backdrop: truly transparent alpha, no colored or white rectangle, no checkerboard texture, no grid lines, no drawn cell boundaries, no button tiles or generic enclosing frames, no base pedestals. No labels, text, numbers, logos or watermark. Requested pictographic heart, cross, question mark, spade, club and checklist marks are permitted symbols.
